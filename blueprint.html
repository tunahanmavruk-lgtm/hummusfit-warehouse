Every Flaw & Assumption — Read Before Building Anything

1
Notch status unconfirmed. The 98"x108" notch in the bottom-left corner is on the original CAD sheet. You raised removing it for dunnage racks, but I never got confirmation it's physically gone. If it's still there, it eats into M3's first ~6 floor positions — check before stocking that row.
2
Door position is an estimate. Confirmed: single blue roll-up door, used for both entry and exit. Not confirmed: exactly where along that wall. Drawn here at roughly 1/3 down the wall — measure before framing the entry spine.
3
Columns are evenly-spaced guesses, not a survey. Site photos show ~4 structural steel columns near the room's centerline. Positions here assume even spacing — the real spots could be anywhere along that line. Nothing gets bolted down, stacked against, or routed through until these are actually measured. Evaporator/fan units appear mounted near them — leave the top crate tier clear underneath for airflow regardless of exact position.
4
Drain positions are approximate. From photos only. The dunnage-rack plan assumes crates can stack over them without blocking drainage — confirm rack clearance height against the lowest crate opening before committing to rack purchases.
5
Aisle widths (48") and the entry spine (60") are planning placeholders, not tested clearances. Before this gets built, physically tape off a 48" lane and push the actual pick cart through it — confirm it clears and can maneuver. Narrow it only if you're sure two-way traffic isn't needed there.
6
Demand data is one Monday, not a verified pattern. Crate counts and lane priority are built on a real 178-order Monday shipout batch, cross-checked against blended Shopify sales. That's dramatically better than blended sales alone (which overstated real demand by ~6x), but it's still a single day — no day-of-week variance is captured. A Saturday batch would meaningfully tighten this.
7
17 of 120 products never appeared in the real order sample. They may be genuinely slow-moving, or they may have simply not shipped that Monday. Their crate counts use a corrected (discounted) blended estimate, not real data — treat those specific lanes as the least trustworthy numbers in the whole build sheet.
8
The whole room survey is photo-based, not measured. Only the outer room dimensions (60'-11 5/8" x 21'-11 1/2") and ceiling height (10') are treated as hard numbers here. Everything else on this page — door, columns, drains, notch, aisle widths — needs a walkthrough with an actual tape measure before anything is bolted, painted, or ordered.
